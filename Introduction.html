Four questions

Scope of Analytics

Descriptive

What happened?

Summarizing and reporting on data to understand past performance.

Diagnostic

Why did it happen?

Examining data to uncover the causes behind past outcomes.

Predictive

What could happen?

Using data and models to forecast future outcomes and patterns.

Prescriptive

What should we do?

Using models and optimization to recommend the best course of action.
